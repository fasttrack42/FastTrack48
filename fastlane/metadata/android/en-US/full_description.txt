Free and fully open source. No ads, no in-app purchases, no Internet permissions.

FastTrack48 tells you what changes are happening to your body while you fast, and you'll learn more
about each phase as you progress:

8-12 Hours: Gluconeogenesis
12-18 Hours: Fat Burning
18-24 Hours: Ketosis
24-48 Hours: Autophagy
48-54 Hours: Increased Growth Hormones
54-72 Hours: Insulin Sensitivity
72 Hours and Beyond: Immune Cell Regeneration

WHILE YOU FAST
• A radial dial showing exactly where you are on the 0 to 72h+ timeline, with a tappable pill for every stage
• An ongoing notification with live elapsed time, plus optional alerts the moment you cross into a new stage
• A home screen widget to provide a live progress without having to open the app

YOUR FASTING HISTORY
• Under the Log tab you'll find a calendar that displays your past fasts and stats
• Running totals of the time you have spent in ketosis and in autophagy
• Add, edit or backdate any fast by hand — forget to hit start and nothing is lost

YOUR DATA NEVER LEAVES YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking
• The app does not even request the INTERNET permission — it cannot phone home
• Everything is stored in a local database on your device
• Export the whole logbook to CSV, calendar (.ics) or Activity Streams JSON at any time
• Import your fast history from EasyFast backup .zip files

LOCALIZATION
• Free and open source under the MIT licence, no strings attached
• Material design throughout, with light, dark and system themes and a configurable date and time format
• Available in nine languages

FastTrack48 is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice. The stage timings come from a rule-of-thumb model informed by published research and real-world testing, and they are an approximation: what stage you are actually in depends on your metabolism, your last meal, your activity and much else. Talk to a doctor before fasting, especially if you are pregnant, are taking medication, or have a medical condition.
