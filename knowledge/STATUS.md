## Material findings
- `AUDIT-stage-copy.md`: 9 issues in the current in-app stage copy. The most important: the 54–72 h "insulin sensitivity resets" claim is physiologically backwards in the short term (SCI-90-001, Hoeks 2010).
- Fung's numbers that the primary source contradicts:
  - 382-day fast: blood glucose was ~30 mg/dL, not "well within normal"
  - "75% of brain energy from ketones at day 4" holds only after weeks
  - "muscle untouched until 4% body fat" is wrong
  - the 3 g leucine autophagy claim cites a muscle-growth study
- Internal conflicts (`note: CONFLICT`):
  - CGF-15-005 vs YT-02-001: book vs later advice on break-fast foods
  - YT-05-010: Ramos vs Fung on transdermal magnesium
