# Final cards: Comfort: common, harmless side effects in healthy people and how to ease them. No diagnoses assumed.

### FC-024
title: Tired or dizzy? Try salt
claim: Fatigue, headache or light-headedness while fasting is often low salt and water, not low energy.
why: Falling insulin makes the kidneys flush sodium and water. A pinch of salt usually helps within minutes.
do: Sit down, take a pinch of salt in water, and rise slowly. If it persists, end the fast.
grade: B
safety: caution
when: symptom:dizzy | symptom:fatigue | symptom:headache | stage:24-48
aud: all
from: CGF-00-008, YT-01-011, CGF-15-013

### FC-025
title: Loose stools early on
claim: Some people get loose stools in early fasts as the body sheds retained water. It usually settles within days.
why: Falling insulin releases water the body was holding; some leaves through the bowel instead of the kidneys.
do: Psyllium husk in water can help. Persistent diarrhea or signs of dehydration mean stop and seek advice.
grade: D
safety: caution
when: symptom:diarrhea | start | stage:24-48
aud: all
from: YT-02-019

### FC-026
title: Heartburn while fasting
claim: Heartburn can worsen on an empty stomach, though for some it improves. Weight loss usually relieves reflux over time.
why: The stomach still makes acid with nothing to absorb it, and belly fat presses upward.
do: Finish eating 3 hours before bed, walk after dinner, raise the head of the bed. Ask about antacids if needed.
grade: B
safety: caution
when: symptom:heartburn | stage:12-20
aud: all
from: CGF-10-007

### FC-027
title: Magnesium: pick the right form
claim: Muscle cramps while fasting can mean low magnesium. If you supplement, choose citrate or glycinate over oxide.
why: Blood tests miss low tissue magnesium. Oxide is poorly absorbed and mostly acts as a laxative.
grade: B
safety: caution
when: symptom:cramps | long
aud: all
from: YT-03-003, CGF-14-004, YT-03-004

### FC-066
title: First-day headache?
claim: A headache in your first fasts is usually low salt, and it fades within a couple of weeks of regular fasting.
why: Going from salty meals to almost no salt changes fluid balance; the body adapts within a few fasts.
do: Add a pinch of salt to your water.
grade: D
safety: none
when: symptom:headache | start | stage:12-20
aud: beginner
from: CGF-15-014, YT-02-018

### FC-150
title: Constipation is normal
claim: Fewer bowel movements while fasting are normal and usually painless. With little food going in, little needs to come out.
why: Discomfort comes from a full colon, which a fasting colon isn't.
do: Eat more vegetables and fiber on eating days; psyllium helps. Ask your doctor if it persists.
grade: B
safety: none
when: symptom:constipation | long
aud: all
from: CGF-14-005

### FC-151
title: Wired at night?
claim: Trouble sleeping or waking early on fasting days is common. Fasting raises energizing noradrenaline.
why: The same hormone that keeps you alert by day can delay sleep. It usually eases with practice.
do: Don't force sleep: wind down without screens, try a warm bath, go to bed when tired. If it persists, shorten or move fasts.
grade: B
safety: none
when: symptom:insomnia | stage:20-24 | stage:24-48
aud: all
from: YT-02-020, YT-05-010

### FC-152
title: Feeling cold
claim: Feeling cold early in fasting is common and usually fades as your body gets better at burning fat. Layer up, drink hot, move.
why: New fasters can lag in switching to fat; movement uses circulating fuel and makes heat.
do: Cold all the time even when eating? Ask your doctor to check your thyroid.
grade: D
safety: none
when: symptom:cold | stage:24-48 | long
aud: all
from: YT-08-003, YT-08-004
