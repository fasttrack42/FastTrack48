### FC-022
title: Trying to conceive
claim: Trying to conceive? Avoid extended fasts after ovulation, since you may already be pregnant. Focus on full, nourishing meals instead.
why: Pregnancy is a reason not to fast. In the second half of the cycle you can't know yet, so shorter eating windows are the safer choice.
grade: D
safety: caution
when: plan | long
aud: women
from: YT-08-005

### FC-120
title: Fasting with your cycle
claim: Appetite is lower in the first half of the cycle and higher after ovulation. Plan longer fasts for days 2–14.
why: Estrogen suppresses appetite; progesterone stimulates it, and insulin sensitivity dips before your period, which makes that week the hardest.
do: In the week or two before your period, prefer shorter fasts and skip sweeteners between meals.
grade: B
safety: none
when: plan | between
aud: women
from: YT-04-007, YT-08-006

### FC-121
title: Weigh by the cycle
claim: Women's weight loss runs in a monthly wave. Compare weight at the same point in each cycle, not week to week.
why: Water retention rises in the second half of the cycle and drops after menstruation, masking fat loss.
grade: B
safety: none
when: plateau
aud: women
from: YT-06-003

### FC-122
title: Perimenopause shifts the rules
claim: In the years before menopause, women gain about 0.5 kg a year and lose lean mass. It's hormonal, not a lack of discipline.
why: Falling estrogen raises appetite and moves fat toward the belly; poor sleep speeds it. After menopause heart risk climbs toward men's.
do: Prioritize strength training, protein and sleep; fasting helps, but expect slower progress.
grade: A
safety: none
when: plateau | onboarding
aud: women
from: YT-04-010, YT-06-005

### FC-124
title: Women benefit too
claim: Women lose weight with fasting at about the same rate as men; a 3-day fast left reproductive hormones normal in normal-weight women.
why: Cycle problems come from low body fat and energy deficiency, not fasting itself, in women with adequate reserves.
grade: B
counter: Lean, very active women face higher risk of energy deficiency; long-term women-specific data are limited.
safety: none
when: myth | onboarding
aud: women
from: CGF-10-008, YT-04-011
