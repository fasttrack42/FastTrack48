# Final cards: Traditional fast days: context for the lunar fast-day feature. Visible only when fast days are enabled.

### FC-020
title: Keep dry fasts short
claim: Fasting without water is harder and riskier and adds no benefit. Keep dry fasts within a day, as in religious practice.
why: Without water the body dehydrates alongside fasting, straining circulation and kidneys and limiting how long a fast can safely run, with no added metabolic benefit.
do: Drink well in the hours before and after a dry fast.
grade: B
safety: caution
when: fastday | fluids | plan
aud: all, t2d, meds
from: CGF-01-020, YT-01-008

### FC-160
title: An ancient calendar
claim: Nearly every tradition sets regular fast days: lunar and weekday fasts in Hinduism, Orthodox Lent, Ramadan, Buddhist monks eating nothing after noon.
why: Long before metabolism was understood, cultures paired feasting with fasting as cleansing and discipline. Crete, the Mediterranean-diet model, fasted on many days a year.
grade: D
safety: none
when: fastday | history
aud: all
from: CGF-02-001, CGF-02-002, CGF-02-004
