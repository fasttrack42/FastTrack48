### YT-02-001
claim: After fasts over 24 hours, have a small "pre-meal" 30 minutes before the main meal: a little fish or meat, cooked vegetables, or avocado.
why: Digestion slows during a fast. A small, easy first portion wakes it gently and takes the edge off appetite before the full meal.
do: Prefer cooked over raw vegetables at first; a glass of water with soaked chia also works.

### YT-02-005
claim: Refeeding risk also involves thiamine (vitamin B1). Long-term antacid or diuretic users, elderly people eating poorly, and patients recovering from surgery face extra risk.
why: Magnesium or aluminium antacids bind phosphorus; diuretics drain potassium and magnesium. Refeeding then pulls these minerals into cells, so blood levels crash further.
do: On diuretics or daily antacids, don't do multi-day fasts without your doctor checking your blood levels.
grade: A
src: YT PVJCdREj4Og; NICE CG32
safety: stop
when: long | break | onboarding
aud: meds, all
tier: core

### YT-02-006
claim: Body-fat scales and DEXA scans often report "muscle loss" during fasting that is really water and glycogen loss.
why: Bioimpedance and DEXA estimate lean mass assuming a stable body-water content. Fasting sheds water, which these methods read as lost lean tissue, and it returns when you eat.
do: Track strength or a tape measure rather than a smart scale's muscle reading during fasting periods.
grade: B
src: YT 5W7i5TWa2Y8; CGF intro
safety: none
when: myth | long
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-02-007
claim: In a 10-day medically supervised fast (~250 kcal/day), grip strength held steady and leg strength rose, while hunger dropped over the first five days.
why: Stair-climb times were unchanged during the fast and improved after refeeding. Noradrenaline likely boosts muscle performance short term.
grade: B
src: YT 5W7i5TWa2Y8; Laurens 2021 J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle (PMID 34668663)
counter: Small, healthy men; some lean tissue was lost. The study shows function is preserved, not zero protein loss.
safety: none
when: long | stage:72+ | myth
aud: all
tier: core
note: VERIFIED citation.

### YT-02-008
claim: Fung argues most people need far less protein than popular advice suggests, and that much of extra protein simply becomes glucose.
why: His view: the body recycles most amino acids from its own protein turnover, so daily net losses are small and excess intake is converted for fuel.
grade: D
src: YT 5W7i5TWa2Y8

### YT-02-009
claim: In a 2024 randomized trial, heart-attack patients who did intermittent fasting recovered more heart pumping function than those on a regular diet.
why: INTERFAST-MI: 48 patients after a major heart attack; ejection fraction improved more with fasting at 4 weeks. Fung cites it as evidence fasting doesn't erode heart muscle.
grade: B
src: YT 5W7i5TWa2Y8; Dutzmann 2024 Circ Heart Fail (PMID 38695175)
counter: Small single-center pilot, not blinded. Heart patients should fast only with their cardiologist's approval.
safety: caution

aud: meds

### YT-02-020
claim: Trouble sleeping or waking early on fasting days is common, a side effect of the energizing hormones fasting releases.
why: Noradrenaline rises during fasting, so you may feel alert at bedtime. Fung frames it as extra energy, not a problem.
