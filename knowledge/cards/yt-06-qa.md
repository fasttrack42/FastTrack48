# YT batch 6: Fasting Method Q&As
# 2VyDdGDXnk8 (Women & fasting: Ramos + Pateguana) · IsijfWT_O8A (Fung Q&A: gallstones, ketones, stuck glucose)

### YT-06-001
claim: PCOS is the most common hormonal disorder in women of reproductive age, and insulin resistance drives it, including in lean women.
why: High insulin pushes the ovaries to make excess androgens and disrupts ovulation. Diagnosis needs two of three: androgen signs, polycystic ovaries, irregular or absent cycles.
do: Irregular cycles, acne or excess hair with weight gain around the middle? Ask your doctor about PCOS and insulin resistance.
grade: A
src: YT 2VyDdGDXnk8; Brito Pateguana & Fung 2020 The PCOS Plan
counter: Prevalence is ~6–13% by standard criteria; the 10–30% quoted is higher than most estimates.
safety: none
when: onboarding
aud: women
tier: core

### YT-06-002
claim: Starting fasting can shift your cycle for the first few months. Occasional delays are common; months without a period are not.
why: Any big change in diet, weight or stress can move ovulation. Fasting Method coaches report cycles usually settle within 3–4 months.
do: Track your cycle. If periods stop for 3 months or more (and you're not pregnant), stop fasting and see your doctor.
grade: D
src: YT 2VyDdGDXnk8
safety: caution
when: between | long
aud: women
tier: core
note: Refines CGF-10-009 (stop on amenorrhea) with the normal-variation nuance.

why: Water retention rises in the second half of the cycle and drops after menstruation, so week-to-week changes mislead.

### YT-06-004

grade: B

### YT-06-006
claim: There's no point eating fat you're not hungry for. "Fat bombs" and butter-loaded drinks add directly to body fat.
why: Dietary fat is absorbed and stored without the insulin step that carbohydrate needs. Natural fat dressing a meal is fine; extra fat for its own sake isn't.

### YT-06-007
claim: For prediabetes you can cut a carb's insulin effect without cutting carbs: less processed versions, cooked-then-cooled starch, vinegar, eat carbs last, earlier in the day.
why: Same food, different processing and context: steel-cut oats spike glucose less than instant; cooling cooked rice or potatoes creates resistant starch.
do: Pick one or two levers at each meal; combined, they substantially blunt the spike.
grade: B

### YT-06-008

why: The fast glucose spike from refined carbs triggers a crash and hunger by mid-morning, which drives extra eating. Processing, not calories, set off the chain.

### YT-06-009
claim: If fasting stops bringing your blood sugar down, ask your doctor about LADA, a slow-onset autoimmune diabetes often mistaken for type 2.
why: In LADA insulin is low, not high, so fasting (which works by lowering insulin) helps far less, and ketoacidosis becomes a risk.
do: A fasting insulin or C-peptide test and diabetes antibody tests can tell them apart.
grade: B
src: YT IsijfWT_O8A
safety: caution
when: plateau | long | between
aud: t2d
tier: core

### YT-06-010
claim: Fasting is a stress, like exercise. The right dose strengthens you; too much on top of life stress can push blood sugar the wrong way.
why: Fasting raises cortisol to release stored sugar. With heavy emotional stress it can overwhelm the benefit; backing off can then improve results.
do: In a high-stress period, shorten fasts rather than pushing harder.
grade: D
src: YT IsijfWT_O8A; CGF intro
safety: none
when: plateau | plan | long
aud: all
tier: core
note: Fung here says fasting raises cortisol; in CGF ch5 he says short fasts don't. Both fit the evidence: short fasts show little change, longer fasts amplify cortisol (see CGF-05-012 counter).

### YT-06-011
claim: Rapid weight loss raises the risk of gallstones. Eat some natural fat at meals to keep the gallbladder emptying.
why: Bile left sitting in an unused gallbladder can crystallize into stones. Very low-fat diets and fast weight loss both increase stasis.
do: With known gallstones or past gallbladder attacks, check with your doctor before long fasts. Shorter fasts with normal meals are lower risk.
grade: B
src: YT IsijfWT_O8A; Erlinger 2000 Eur J Gastroenterol Hepatol
counter: Fung says there are no good fasting-specific studies; rapid-weight-loss data come from very low-calorie diets and bariatric surgery.
safety: caution
when: onboarding | long
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-06-012
claim: How quickly ketones rise in a first long fast varies widely: some people are in deep ketosis within a day, others take about three.
why: "Metabolic flexibility," the speed of switching from glucose to fat, differs by fitness and metabolic health. Slow switchers feel a low-energy gap.
do: If you're a slow switcher, build up with shorter fasts and lower-carb eating; flexibility improves with practice.
grade: C
src: YT IsijfWT_O8A
safety: none
when: long | stage:24-48 | symptom:fatigue
aud: all
tier: tail

### YT-06-013
claim: In rats, fasting slowed cyst growth in polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD). Whether it works in people isn't known yet.
why: Fasting switches off insulin and mTOR growth signaling; the fast-growing kidney cysts appear to respond.
grade: C
src: YT IsijfWT_O8A
counter: Animal data plus early human pilots. Kidney patients need their nephrologist's guidance (YT-04-004).
