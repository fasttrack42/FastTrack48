# SCI iteration 2: targeted gap-filling (early stages, medications, GLP-1, kidney stones, older adults)

### SCI-91-001
claim: After a meal, blood sugar peaks within about an hour and insulin rises with it. While insulin is high, fat burning is paused.
why: Insulin moves incoming glucose into cells and stores the surplus as glycogen and fat, and it shuts off fat release from fat cells until levels fall.
grade: A
src: Cahill 2006 Annu Rev Nutr
safety: none
when: stage:0-2
aud: all
tier: core

### SCI-91-002
claim: A 10–15 minute easy walk after eating noticeably lowers the blood-sugar and insulin spike from that meal.
why: Working muscles take up glucose without needing much insulin. In a meta-analysis of crossover trials, light walking cut the post-meal rise more than standing.
do: Walk after your biggest meal, especially one with bread, rice, pasta or dessert.
grade: B
src: Buffey 2022 Sports Med (PMID 35147898)
safety: none
when: stage:0-2 | eat | break
aud: all, t2d
tier: core

### SCI-91-003
claim: Ravenous 3–4 hours after a sugary or starchy meal? That's often a blood-sugar rebound, not a real shortage of fuel.
why: A fast glucose spike calls up a big insulin surge that can overshoot, pushing glucose below baseline a few hours later and triggering hunger.
do: Ride it out with water or tea; next time eat protein and vegetables first and carbs last.
grade: B
src: Shukla 2019 Diabetes Obes Metab (PMID 30101510); Ludwig 1999 Pediatrics
safety: none
when: stage:2-5 | hunger
aud: all
tier: core

### SCI-91-004
claim: Ghrelin, the hunger hormone, rises before your usual mealtimes and then falls whether you eat or not. Hunger at noon is partly a clock signal.
why: In people eating on a schedule, ghrelin climbed in the hour before each habitual meal and fell afterward: a learned, anticipatory pattern.
grade: B
src: Cummings 2001 Diabetes (PMID 11473029)
safety: none
when: stage:2-5 | stage:5-8 | hunger
aud: all
tier: core

### SCI-91-005
claim: A few hours after a meal (roughly 3–5, longer with insulin resistance), insulin settles back to baseline and fat cells start releasing fat again.
why: With insulin low, the brake on fat release lifts; the liver begins drawing on glycogen. A snack now raises insulin again and pushes this switch back.
do: Let this window happen: no snacks between meals.
grade: B
src: Cahill 2006 Annu Rev Nutr; CGF ch1
safety: none
when: stage:5-8 | between
aud: all
tier: core

### SCI-91-006
claim: Weight-loss drugs like semaglutide or tirzepatide work; fasting on top needs care: protein, strength training and fluids matter more, not longer fasts.
why: These drugs already cut appetite steeply, so muscle loss and dehydration (from nausea or vomiting) become the risks. With insulin or sulfonylureas, lows become likelier.
do: Plan fasting with your prescriber. Make every meal protein-first, lift 2–3×/week, drink enough.
grade: B
src: Ramadan GLP-1RA systematic review 2025 World J Methodol (PMID 40900863)
counter: In Ramadan studies GLP-1 drugs caused fewer lows than other diabetes drugs but more gastrointestinal effects.
safety: caution
when: onboarding | plan | long
aud: meds, t2d
tier: core

### SCI-91-007
claim: A year after stopping semaglutide, people had regained about two-thirds of the weight they'd lost.
why: The drug suppresses appetite only while it's taken. When it stops, hunger and the body's defended weight return unless habits have changed.
do: If you use one of these drugs, use the time to build your eating rhythm (meals, no snacks, fasting days) for when you stop.
grade: A
src: Wilding 2022 Diabetes Obes Metab (STEP 1 extension, PMID 35441470)
safety: none
when: onboarding | between
aud: meds, all
tier: core

### SCI-91-008
claim: On lithium, dehydration or a sudden drop in salt intake can raise lithium to toxic levels. Fasting needs your psychiatrist's agreement and good hydration.
why: The kidneys handle lithium like sodium; when salt and water fall, they retain lithium. A Ramadan study found levels stayed stable in well-controlled patients.
grade: B
src: Farooq 2010 Int Clin Psychopharmacol (PMID 20827213)
safety: caution
when: onboarding | fluids
aud: meds
tier: core

### SCI-91-009
claim: If you take steroid replacement (adrenal insufficiency) or long-term prednisone, don't fast without your endocrinologist, and never skip a dose.
why: Without enough cortisol the body can't keep blood sugar and blood pressure up during a fast; adrenal crisis is a real risk.
grade: A
src: Chihaoui 2021 Endocrine (PMID 34213700)
safety: stop
when: onboarding | plan
aud: meds
tier: core

### SCI-91-010
claim: On warfarin, big changes in eating (fasting, far more or fewer greens) can shift your INR. Check it more often when you start.
why: Warfarin's effect depends on steady vitamin K intake and on liver metabolism; abrupt dietary change alters both.
grade: B
src: Ansell 2008 Chest
safety: caution
when: onboarding | plan
aud: meds
tier: core

### SCI-91-011
claim: Morning fasting suits levothyroxine (thyroid hormone): it's absorbed best on an empty stomach, 30–60 minutes before food.
why: Food, calcium and iron all reduce absorption; a long fasted morning makes consistent dosing easier.
grade: A
src: Jonklaas 2014 Thyroid
safety: none
when: start | fluids
aud: meds
tier: tail

### SCI-91-012
claim: Had kidney stones? Fasting and ketosis raise uric acid and concentrate urine; ~6% on long-term ketogenic diets developed stones, mostly uric acid.
why: Ketones compete with uric acid for excretion, and low fluid intake concentrates the urine, both favoring stone formation.
do: Drink well (pale urine), and talk to your doctor about longer fasts if you've had stones.
grade: B
src: Acharya 2021 Diseases (PMID 34070285); CGF ch10
safety: caution
when: onboarding | long | fluids
aud: all
tier: core

### SCI-91-013
claim: Over 65, weight loss takes muscle and bone unless you protect them: shorter fasts, protein every meal, strength training, stand up slowly.
why: Older muscle responds less to protein, and dieting older adults lose lean mass and bone; adding resistance plus aerobic exercise preserved them in an RCT.
grade: A
src: Villareal 2017 N Engl J Med
safety: caution
when: onboarding | plan | between
aud: all
tier: core
