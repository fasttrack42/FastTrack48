# CGF ch4: The Advantages of Fasting

### CGF-04-001
claim: The whole method fits in one sentence: eat nothing; drink water or tea.
why: Diets often fail because they're confusing to follow. A rule this simple is hard to get wrong, and adherence is what produces results.
grade: D
src: CGF ch4
safety: none
when: onboarding | start | fluids
aud: beginner
tier: core

### CGF-04-002
claim: Fasting costs nothing and saves time: no shopping, cooking or cleanup.
why: Healthy food can be expensive and time-consuming. Fasting removes both barriers and works for people on any budget.
grade: D
src: CGF ch4; YT tIuj-oMN-Fk
safety: none
when: plan
aud: all
tier: tail

### CGF-04-003
claim: Feasts and fasts are meant to alternate. A planned fast after a celebration lets you enjoy it without guilt.
why: Humans have always paired feast days with fasting. Balance over the week matters more than any single meal.
do: Enjoy the wedding or holiday meal, then schedule a fast in the days after. Don't let a feast end your routine.
grade: D
src: CGF ch4
safety: none
when: between | plan | fastday
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-04-004
claim: Fasting is fully flexible: move a fast day, shorten it, or stop within minutes if you feel unwell.
why: Unlike surgery or fixed meal plans, nothing is locked in. Rescheduling around important days keeps fasting sustainable.
do: If a fast clashes with an important meeting or event, move it; consistency over weeks beats rigid days.
grade: D
src: CGF ch4
safety: none
when: plan | between
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-04-006
claim: When weight stalls, Fung's lever is fasting "dose": longer or more frequent fasts, rather than switching diets.
why: Low-carb or low-fat diets bottom out at zero carbs or fat; fasting time can be extended until the effect appears.
do: Plateaued? Add one longer fast a week or another fasting day before changing your whole approach.
grade: D
src: CGF ch4
counter: Risk rises with duration. Beyond about 3 days, medical supervision applies (see SCI-02-001). Fung's "no upper limit" is a therapeutic framing, not a safety claim.
safety: none
when: plateau
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-04-007
claim: Fung lists fasting as preventing Alzheimer's, extending life and reversing aging.
why: These rest on animal studies and mechanisms (autophagy, insulin, inflammation) rather than human outcome trials.
grade: C
src: CGF ch4
counter: No human trial shows fasting prevents dementia or extends lifespan. Don't present these as established benefits.
safety: none
when: myth
aud: advanced
tier: drop
note: Keep for audit. Any in-app longevity copy must be framed as "studied in animals".

### CGF-04-008
claim: Sustained intermittent fasting typically lowers inflammation (CRP), blood sugar, HbA1c and blood pressure.
why: Fat loss plus long low-insulin periods improve the main markers of metabolic health.
grade: A
src: CGF ch4; de Cabo & Mattson 2019 N Engl J Med
counter: Effects are largely explained by weight loss; versus equal-calorie daily restriction, results are similar.
safety: none
when: between | onboarding
aud: all
tier: core
