# CGF Part III: Resources (Fasting Fluids, Protocols)

### CGF-16-001
claim: Flavor fasting water with lemon, lime, fruit slices (not eaten), vinegar, a pinch of salt, or a spoon of chia or ground flax.
why: These add taste, salt or fiber with negligible energy. Sweetened powders and drops, even sugar-free, are out.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: none
when: fluids
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-16-003
claim: Herbal teas are unlimited while fasting. Peppermint eases gas and bloating; cinnamon chai can curb sweet cravings.
why: Herbal infusions add warmth, ritual and flavor with no energy, so they suit any hour of the fasting day.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: none
when: fluids | hunger | symptom:bloating
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-16-004
claim: Bitter melon tea, cinnamon and black or oolong teas are traditionally used to lower blood sugar. Take care if you're on diabetes drugs.
why: Their glucose-lowering effect adds to medication's, raising the risk of lows during a fast.
do: On insulin or diabetes pills, check with your doctor before using bitter melon regularly.
grade: C
src: CGF resources
counter: Human evidence for most of these is weak; bitter melon has the most data, with modest effects.
safety: caution
when: fluids
aud: t2d, meds
tier: core

### CGF-16-006
claim: Fung's 24-hour protocol: three dinner-to-dinner (or lunch-to-lunch) fasts a week, plus a daily 16:8 on other days. It suits steady weight loss.
why: A meal every day keeps medications and family dinners possible; two fasts a week works for a gentler pace.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: none
when: plan
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-16-007
claim: Fung's 36-hour protocol: three full non-eating days a week (dinner to breakfast two days later). Stronger for weight and prediabetes than the 24-hour plan.
why: Longer daily stretches of low insulin lower blood sugar more; some people find a full day off food simpler than one meal.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: none
when: plan
aud: all
tier: core

### CGF-16-008
claim: Fung's 42-hour protocol, for type 2 diabetes: three full fasting days a week plus no breakfast on any day (dinner to lunch two days later).
why: The extra hours give blood glucose and insulin more time to fall. Medication must be adjusted beforehand to avoid lows.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: caution
when: plan
aud: t2d, meds
tier: core

### CGF-16-009
claim: 7–14-day fasts are a supervised clinical protocol: physician oversight, adjusted medication, a daily multivitamin, and blood tests.
why: Fung uses them for severe diabetes or obesity, limited to once a month (7 days) or every six weeks (14 days) because of refeeding risk.
do: Don't attempt fasts beyond ~3 days without your doctor's involvement.
grade: D
src: CGF resources
safety: stop
when: plan | long
aud: all
tier: core
