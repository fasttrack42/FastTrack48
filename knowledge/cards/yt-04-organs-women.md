### YT-04-001
claim: Fatty liver affects roughly a quarter to a third of adults, mostly without symptoms. It's driven largely by sugar and refined starch, not dietary fat.
why: The liver turns excess glucose and especially fructose into new fat (de novo lipogenesis). In overfeeding studies, a 2% weight gain came with ~27% more liver fat.
grade: B
src: YT _oBkdmsRu0Y; Sevastianova 2012 Am J Clin Nutr
safety: none
when: onboarding | eat
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-04-002
claim: Liver fat responds fast. Teenage boys who cut added sugar to under 3% of calories reduced liver fat by about a third in 8 weeks.
why: Removing fructose shuts down much of the liver's new-fat production; the stored fat is then exported and burned.
grade: A
src: YT _oBkdmsRu0Y; Schwimmer 2019 JAMA
safety: none
when: eat | between
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-04-003
claim: In a randomized trial, both 5:2 fasting and a low-carb diet more than halved liver fat in people with fatty liver over 12 weeks.
why: Both cut the insulin and sugar load that drives liver fat production. The standard-advice group improved far less.
grade: B
src: YT _oBkdmsRu0Y; Holmer 2021 JHEP Rep (PMID 33898960)
safety: none
when: plan | between
aud: all, t2d
tier: core
note: VERIFIED citation.

### YT-04-004
claim: With kidney disease, fast only under your nephrologist. Early diabetic kidney damage may improve with periodic fasting, but electrolyte and medication risks are higher.
why: In a 6-month trial, monthly 5-day fasting-mimicking cycles lowered HbA1c and insulin resistance and reduced urine protein in early disease.
do: If you have kidney disease or protein in your urine, bring a fasting plan to your kidney doctor rather than starting alone.
grade: B
src: YT SI_c16-_dqw; Sulaj 2022 J Clin Endocrinol Metab
counter: Proof-of-concept, n=40; the overall urine-protein result wasn't statistically significant.
safety: caution
when: onboarding | plan | long
aud: meds, t2d
tier: core

### YT-04-005
claim: Skipping breakfast is the easiest fast; skipping dinner is the most effective.
why: Breakfast is small (~20% of daily intake), little-hungry and rarely social. Dinner is the biggest meal, eaten when insulin responses run highest, but it's the hardest to skip.
do: Default to skipping breakfast; swap in a skipped dinner when you want a stronger effect, such as before an event.
grade: D
src: YT 7iG2tp_BdEM; CGF ch12
safety: none
when: plan
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-04-007
claim: Women's appetite shifts with the cycle: lower in the first half (rising estrogen), higher after ovulation (progesterone). Plan longer fasts for the first two weeks.
why: Estrogen suppresses appetite and progesterone stimulates it; food intake measurably dips around ovulation and rises in the luteal phase.
do: Do longer fasts in days ~1–14; in the week or two before your period, favor shorter fasts or a simple eating window.
grade: B
src: YT o9YXEgMheEo; YT 9j4AX9WxWHY; Buffenstein 1995 Physiol Behav
safety: none
when: plan | between
aud: women
tier: core
note: Phase-2 idea: optional cycle-aware fasting suggestion (opt-in only, privacy-sensitive).

### YT-04-008

why: Once past day 2, hunger tends to fade. Repeated restarts mean repeatedly facing the hardest early hours.

### YT-04-010
claim: In the years before menopause, women gain roughly 0.5 kg a year and lose lean mass. It's a hormonal shift, not a failure of discipline.
why: Falling estrogen raises appetite and moves fat from hips toward the belly (visceral fat); poor sleep, common in perimenopause, speeds the gain.
do: Prioritize strength training, protein and sleep; fasting helps, but expect slower progress during this window.
grade: A
src: YT o9YXEgMheEo; YT 9j4AX9WxWHY; Greendale 2019 JCI Insight
safety: none
when: plateau | onboarding
aud: women
tier: core

### YT-04-012
claim: Protein, fat and fiber trigger your gut's own fullness hormones (GLP-1, GIP), the same pathway Ozempic-type drugs amplify.
why: These nutrients slow stomach emptying and stimulate incretin release; fiber feeds gut bacteria whose fatty acids boost GLP-1 further.
do: Build meals around protein, natural fats and fibrous vegetables to stay full longer.
grade: B
src: YT le3Ifc7D5Vk
counter: Natural GLP-1 rises are far smaller and shorter than drug levels; don't expect drug-like effects.
safety: none
when: eat | break | hunger
aud: all
tier: core

### YT-04-013
claim: Bitter melon and curcumin (turmeric) may raise GLP-1 and lower blood sugar, which may explain their traditional use for diabetes.
why: Bitter-taste receptors in the gut may trigger incretin release; studies of bitter gourd show glucose-lowering effects.
grade: C
src: YT le3Ifc7D5Vk
counter: Small and animal studies. On diabetes medication these can add to the risk of lows (CGF-16-004).

### YT-04-014
claim: Large weight loss tends to remove visceral (organ) fat proportionally faster than fat under the skin, which is the metabolically important part.
why: In a 12-week 600-kcal/day diet, visceral fat fell roughly twice as much in proportion as body weight; time-restricted eating plus low-carb cut visceral fat in an RCT.
