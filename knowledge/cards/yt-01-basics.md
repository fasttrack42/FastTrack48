### YT-01-008
claim: Fung advises keeping dry fasts (no water) under about 16–18 hours.
why: Without fluids you dehydrate alongside fasting, which limits safe duration. Some find short dry fasts ease hunger and frequent urination.
do: For religious dry fasts, keep them within a day, rehydrate well afterwards, and avoid them if you're on diuretics or have kidney problems.

### YT-01-013
claim: The most common fasting mistake is eating too much when you eat: fitting a whole day's food, or junk food, into one meal or window.
why: The fast works by letting stored fat cover the missing meals. If the next meal replaces all of them, there's no deficit left to cover.
do: Eat a normal-sized, whole-food meal after a fast; slightly larger is fine, but not three meals in one sitting.
grade: B
src: YT r6vkEIeBj_E
counter: Fung attributes null RCT results (Trepanowski 2017, Lowe 2020) to this design flaw. But in Liu 2022, with calories matched, time-restricted eating added no benefit over calorie restriction.
safety: none
when: break | eat
aud: all
tier: core
