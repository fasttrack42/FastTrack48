# SCI: findings verified against primary literature (step 4)

### SCI-90-001
claim: Around 2–3 days into a fast, muscles temporarily become less responsive to insulin. This is normal: it saves glucose for the brain.
why: Fat released from stores floods muscle and blocks insulin's glucose uptake. The effect reverses within a day or two of eating.
do: A higher-than-usual glucose reading after a multi-day fast, or right after refeeding, is expected; judge insulin health between fasts.
grade: B
src: Hoeks 2010 Diabetes (PMID 20573749)
counter: n=12 healthy men, 60 h fast. Repeated fasting with fat loss improves insulin sensitivity over weeks, which is the long-term effect Fung describes.
safety: none
when: stage:54-72 | stage:48-54 | break
aud: all, t2d
tier: core
