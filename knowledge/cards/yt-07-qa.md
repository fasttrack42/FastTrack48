### YT-07-001
claim: On thyroid medication, recheck your thyroid levels when you start fasting or lose weight: the dose you need may drop.
why: Weight loss and calmer autoimmune inflammation can lower the requirement; symptoms are a poor guide, so an unchanged dose can tip you into over-replacement.
do: Racing heart, anxiety, heat intolerance or poor sleep while fasting on thyroid pills: get labs checked.
grade: B
src: YT jTgADBtoETY; YT R8CdI_kUD74
counter: Reports of fasting improving Hashimoto's are anecdotal; no trials.
safety: caution
when: onboarding | between | symptom:insomnia
aud: meds
tier: core

### YT-07-002
claim: With Hashimoto's or hypothyroidism, fast when levels are optimal. While your dose is being adjusted, stick to meals without snacks rather than long fasts.
why: Dose changes take ~5–6 weeks to show in labs. Long fasts during that unstable window make symptoms and levels harder to read and correct.
grade: D
src: YT R8CdI_kUD74 (Pateguana & Ramos)
safety: caution
when: plan | long
aud: meds
tier: core

### YT-07-006
claim: Bones get stronger only under load. Walking, stairs and resistance training protect bone far more than calcium pills do.
why: Bone remodels in response to mechanical stress; astronauts lose bone despite eating well. Daily movement matters as much as gym sessions.
do: Combine fasting with regular weight-bearing activity, sun exposure for vitamin D, and nutrient-dense meals.
grade: A
src: YT jTgADBtoETY
counter: Fung's Japan comparison (less calcium, fewer fractures) ignores genetics and hip geometry. Calcium and vitamin D still matter if deficient.
safety: none
when: between | long
aud: all, women
tier: core

### YT-07-007
claim: The 2024 headline "8-hour eating raises heart-death risk by 91%" came from an unreviewed conference abstract based on two days of diet recall.
why: Participants weren't intentionally fasting, eating windows were self-reported twice over years, and confounders were unclear. That's weak evidence for harm.
grade: B
src: YT jTgADBtoETY; Chen 2024 Circulation (abstract P192)
counter: AHA's own reviewer (C. Gardner) flagged the same limits: two days of diet recall, no diet-quality or baseline comparison. Check for a later peer-reviewed version before quoting numbers.
safety: none
when: myth
aud: all
tier: core
note: VERIFIED via AHA newsroom (18 Mar 2024): NHANES 2003–18, ~20,000 adults, two 24-h recalls, preliminary and not peer-reviewed.

### YT-07-009
claim: Don't drink alcohol during a fast. It blocks the liver's glucose production, so blood sugar can drop, and it hits much harder.
why: The liver prioritizes clearing alcohol and stops gluconeogenesis, so lows can follow, especially on diabetes drugs. An empty stomach raises blood alcohol fast.
do: If you drink, have it with a meal on an eating day, and drink water alongside.
grade: A
src: YT R8CdI_kUD74
safety: stop
when: fluids | start | stage:12-20 | stage:24-48
aud: all, meds, t2d
tier: core

### YT-07-011
claim: With diabetes or on medication, religious dry fasts need a plan from your doctor, and many traditions allow exemptions or water for health reasons.
why: Water and food restriction together raise risks of dehydration, low blood sugar and low blood pressure; Ramadan guidelines stratify risk and adjust medications.
grade: A
src: YT R8CdI_kUD74; IDF-DAR 2021 Diabetes and Ramadan guidelines
